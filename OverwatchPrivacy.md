
Overwatch — Privacy Policy

Developed by D3 Digital
Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Overview

D3 Digital respects the privacy of Overwatch users and their company information.

Overwatch is an amusement ride inspection and maintenance management application designed to help ride owners and operators maintain operational records.

Information Stored

Overwatch allows users to create and maintain information including:

* Company and equipment information
* Inspection and maintenance records
* Employee and training records
* Photographs, documents, and signatures

This information is entered and managed by the user.

Data Storage and iCloud Synchronization

Overwatch uses Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit services to synchronize information between compatible devices and authorized users.

Company information is maintained through Apple’s infrastructure.

D3 Digital does not independently receive, collect, or have access to customers’ company records.

D3 Digital can access company information only when a customer explicitly chooses to share that information with D3 Digital, using the same sharing functionality available to other authorized users.

Data Sharing

Overwatch allows users to share company information with other authorized users through Apple’s CloudKit sharing functionality.

D3 Digital does not sell customer information or share it with advertisers.

Analytics and Tracking

Overwatch does not incorporate third-party advertising, analytics, or user-tracking services.

Data Management

Users are responsible for managing their company information and determining which individuals are authorized to access shared records.
